Papermail

An email app for macOS.

Papermail reads and sends mail for Gmail, Outlook and iCloud accounts. It talks to your mail provider directly from your Mac and stores your mail there, in a local database.

Papermail has no server of its own. Nobody but you and your mail provider handles your mail, unless you switch on AI features with your own AI provider key.

Gmail access

When you add a Gmail account, Google asks you to let Papermail read, organise and send your mail. Papermail needs these permissions to show your inbox, archive and label conversations, mark them read, and send replies.
